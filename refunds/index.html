Refund Policy

We review billing problems using the purchase, provisioning and service records linked to your Pikvaa account.

Effective date: 8 November 2026.

This policy has two parts: what studios pay Pikvaa (plans and event packs), and what clients pay a studio through Pikvaa for a shoot booking.

Part A - Studio plans and event packs

You may request a refund review within 7 days of a charge if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
  • payment succeeded but the plan or event pack was not activated within 24 hours; or
  • a verified Pikvaa service problem prevented meaningful use and support could not fix it within 3 business days.

A change of mind after the plan or pack has been used, unused storage or capacity, missed client deadlines, storage that expired as shown on the event screen, files deleted after the displayed recovery period, or problems outside Pikvaa's control do not normally qualify. Rights you have under Indian consumer law are not affected.

Cancelling a plan

You can cancel renewal at any time from your account. The current plan stays active until the end of the paid period and no further charge is attempted. Cancelling does not refund the current period or an event pack already activated. Downgrades take effect from the next billing period.

Part B - Client payments for a shoot booking

When a client pays a booking advance to a studio through Pikvaa, the following default applies unless the studio's booking confirmation shows a different rule:

  • Client cancels 7 or more days before the shoot date: the full advance is refunded.
  • Client cancels less than 7 days before the shoot date: the advance is non-refundable.
  • Studio cancels: the client receives a full refund of everything paid for that booking.

A studio may change the refund amount for a specific booking, for example to offer a reschedule credit or a partial refund. Every such change is recorded with the reason, the person who made it and the time in the booking's audit log, and the client is shown the final amount.

The photography service contract is between the studio and its client. Questions about the shoot, deliverables or a refund amount should go to the studio first. If the studio does not respond within 7 days, write to Pikvaa and we will help both sides using the booking and payment records.

How to request a refund

Studios: open Help in your Pikvaa account, choose Contact support, then Billing. Clients: use the cancel or contact option on your booking page. Include the studio name, booking or transaction reference, date and a short description. Never send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or full card details.

Timelines and method

We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method through our payment processor, Razorpay, and usually reach the account within 5-7 business days, depending on the bank. Payment gateway charges already deducted by the processor on a studio-initiated refund may not be recoverable; the studio is shown this before confirming.

Payment disputes

Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank so we can resolve it quickly. We may pause an account while a chargeback on a Pikvaa charge is under review.
