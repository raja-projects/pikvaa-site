Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations and refunds work for storage packs and Pro.

This website and the Pikvaa app are operated by Tanishkha (Pikvaa is a product of Tanishkha), Ground Floor, 3-158, Middle Street, Swamythoppu, Kamarajapuram, Suchindrum, Kanniyakumari, Tamil Nadu - 629704, India. On this page, "Pikvaa", "we" and "us" mean that business.

Storage packs (prepaid)

  • Storage packs are prepaid, one-time purchases for a single event, valid for the period shown at purchase (6 or 12 months).
  • You can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if no photos have been uploaded to the pack.
  • Once photos have been uploaded or shared with a client from a pack, the pack is considered used and is not refundable.
  • Upgrades and top-ups are charged as the price difference and follow the same rule.
  • Unused time remaining on a pack is not refunded.

Pro plan (yearly)

  • You can cancel Pro at any time from your account; it stays active until the end of the paid year and will not renew.
  • A full refund is available within 7 days of the first payment for a Pro year. After that, part-used years are not refunded.
  • The 30-day trial is free; you are not charged unless you choose to buy.

Failed or duplicate payments

If you were charged twice, or charged but the purchase did not activate, email us with the payment ID and we will refund the extra or failed charge in full.

How to request a refund

Email hello@pikvaa.com from your registered email, with your payment ID and reason. We reply within 2 business days.

Refund timelines

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.
