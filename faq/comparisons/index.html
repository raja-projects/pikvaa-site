Comparisons

How is Pikvaa different from Pixieset?

Pixieset is a polished all-in-one client gallery and online store platform built mainly for Western photographers, with monthly or yearly plans. Pikvaa is built for Indian studios: per-event packs in rupees from ₹99 before GST, Tamil and English, WhatsApp sharing, joint family selection with OTP submit, UPI payment records and payment-linked release.

Is Pikvaa a good Kwikpic alternative in India?

It depends on your need. Kwikpic focuses on AI face-search event photo sharing, where guests find their own photos. Pikvaa focuses on the studio workflow: album selection by the family, watermarked previews, payment records and release only after payment, plus CRM, crew and bookings. Pikvaa does not offer face search.

Why use Pikvaa instead of Google Drive to deliver photos?

Google Drive is general file storage, so studios still manage selection over WhatsApp, can't easily lock full quality until payment and often share open links. Pikvaa adds PIN-protected watermarked previews, family selection with tags and album limits, OTP-locked submission, payment tracking and controlled downloads, with clear expiry dates per event.

Are Google Drive photos safe for client delivery?

Google Drive is reliable storage, but a shared link can be forwarded and anyone with access can download full-quality files, with no payment control or album selection. For client work, a gallery app like Pikvaa keeps previews watermarked behind a PIN and releases full quality only after payment and approval.

How is Pikvaa different from WeTransfer?

WeTransfer sends files through expiring links, with no gallery, selection or payment control. Pikvaa gives each event a PIN-protected gallery for viewing and selecting, records part-payments, and releases full-quality downloads only after payment, with clear warnings before the gallery expires.

How does Pikvaa compare with Pic-Time and ShootProof?

Pic-Time is a sales and print-focused gallery platform and ShootProof combines galleries with contracts and invoices; both are built for Western markets with monthly plans. Pikvaa is built for Indian studios, with per-event rupee packs, Tamil and English, WhatsApp sharing, family album selection and UPI payment records. Check their sites for current features.

Is Pikvaa like SmugMug?

Not really. SmugMug is a portfolio and photo-hosting platform with unlimited storage plans and no free plan. Pikvaa is a working tool for client jobs: per-event storage packs, PIN galleries, family album selection, payment records and controlled final delivery, built for Indian studios. Pikvaa never offers unlimited storage.

Does Pikvaa have AI face search like some photo sharing apps?

No. AI face search is not part of Pikvaa's launch features. Pikvaa focuses on album selection by the client's family, watermarked previews, payment-linked release, bookings and crew. If guests finding their own photos by selfie is your main need, a face-search app may suit that job better.

Does Pikvaa offer AI photo culling?

No, AI culling is not part of Pikvaa. You choose which photos to upload from your cards, and the client's family selects album photos with tags and priorities. Pikvaa focuses on selection, payment and delivery rather than editing or culling.

Does Pikvaa sell prints or photo albums online?

No. Pikvaa does not include a print store. It handles the studio workflow up to the album list: the family selects and prioritises photos for the album, and the studio designs and prints the album its usual way. Studios can sell extra social-size downloads beyond the share quota.

Which wedding photo software is cheapest in India?

Prices vary and change often, so compare current plans directly. Pikvaa uses per-event packs instead of monthly fees: Lite 3 GB at ₹99 for 6 months and the 40 GB wedding pack at ₹999 for 6 months, before GST, with a Free plan for delivery. You pay only for events you actually shoot.

How much does a wedding photo sharing app cost in India?

Costs vary by app, from free tiers to yearly plans. With Pikvaa, a wedding usually uses the 40 GB pack at ₹999 for 6 months before GST, about ₹1,179 with GST, or ₹1,378 with Protected backup. Small events can use Lite at ₹99. There is no monthly fee.

Do I need both a gallery app and Google Photos?

For clients, a gallery app handles viewing, selection and paid delivery, while Google Photos or Drive suits personal storage. Many families download their released photos from Pikvaa and then keep a copy in Google Photos or on a drive, which is a good long-term backup.

What is the best app for photographers to share photos with clients in India?

Look for a private gallery clients open without installing anything, family selection, watermarked previews, payment control and rupee pricing. Pikvaa offers all of these, plus WhatsApp sharing, Tamil and English, and per-event packs from ₹99 before GST. Compare a few options against how your studio actually works.
