Pricing and GST

How much does Pikvaa cost?

Pikvaa has a Free plan, an optional yearly Pro plan and per-event storage packs. Packs start at ₹99 for Lite 3 GB for 6 months, the 40 GB wedding pack is ₹999 for 6 months, and the 120 GB pack is ₹2,999 for 6 months. All prices are before 18% GST, paid once per event.

Are Pikvaa prices inclusive of GST?

No. All Pikvaa prices are listed before 18% GST, and GST is added on the invoice. Checkout shows both the price before GST and the GST-inclusive price, so you see exactly what you pay. For example, the 40 GB wedding pack is ₹999 before GST, about ₹1,179 including GST.

Will I get a GST invoice for Pikvaa payments?

Yes. Every payment you make to Pikvaa is stored with its Razorpay payment ID and an invoice, sent to your verified backup email. Our invoices add 18% GST on top of the listed prices. A verified email is required before your first pack payment, so every invoice reaches you.

How much is Pikvaa Pro?

Pikvaa Pro is priced at ₹4,999 per year plus GST, about ₹417 a month, and the price is still being confirmed with photography studios. Pro adds the full CRM, studio branding, five staff accounts and all landing page templates. Extra staff seats are priced at ₹999 per year each. Storage packs are paid separately.

Is Pikvaa a monthly subscription?

No. There is no monthly fee for storage. You pay once per event for a storage pack sized to that job, for 6 or 12 months. The optional Pro plan, which adds the full CRM, staff and branding, is billed yearly. The Free plan costs nothing and still lets you sell packs and deliver galleries.

What is the cheapest way to deliver one small event on Pikvaa?

The Lite pack is the cheapest option: 3 GB per event for ₹99 for 6 months or ₹149 for 12 months, before GST. It suits small shoots such as birthdays or portraits. Photo count depends on file size, so there is no fixed photo-count promise. You can upgrade later by paying the difference.

Do Razorpay fees get added on top of Pikvaa prices?

No. The Razorpay fee is already included in Pikvaa's pack prices, so you only pay the listed price plus 18% GST. Clients never pay through Razorpay at all; they pay your studio directly, usually by UPI, and you record the payment in the app.

Can Pikvaa prices change?

Pikvaa's launch prices are shaped with five to ten photography studios on real paid events, so they may be adjusted before or after launch. Whatever happens, the exact price and expiry of a pack are locked and shown at purchase, so a pack you have already bought never changes price.

Does Pikvaa offer refunds?

Pro is yearly and part-used years are not refunded if you stop renewing; your account simply moves to Free and nothing is deleted. Packs are paid per event and their exact expiry is shown before you pay. For special cases, platform admins can issue goodwill credit or a pack refund, decided individually.

Why does Pikvaa charge per event instead of unlimited storage?

Unlimited storage is not sustainable, so Pikvaa never offers it. Each pack is priced from real storage costs, and you pay only for the space and months an event needs. A small birthday shoot costs ₹99 while a full wedding with video uses a bigger pack, which keeps prices fair for small studios.

How much is the 50 GB Photos plus Clips pack?

The 50 GB Photos plus Clips plus Final pack costs ₹1,249 for 6 months or ₹2,199 for 12 months before 18% GST, about ₹1,474 including GST for 6 months. Protected versions cost ₹1,718 and ₹3,068. It suits weddings where you deliver short clips along with photos.

What is the extra archive month price?

After expiry, files are archived for 30 days. If more archive time is needed, an extra archive month costs ₹9 for Lite, ₹79 for 40 GB, ₹99 for 50 GB and ₹249 for 120 GB, before GST. Restoring a gallery from the archive costs ₹99 plus an extension.
