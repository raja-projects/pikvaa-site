Photography business

How do photographers share photos with clients?

Photographers share photos through online galleries, cloud links like Google Drive or Dropbox, file-transfer services, or pen drives. Online gallery apps are the most client-friendly because clients view on any phone, select favourites and download after payment. Pikvaa adds PIN protection, family selection and WhatsApp sharing for Indian studios.

How do photographers send photos to clients for free?

Free options include Google Drive, free tiers of gallery apps and WeTransfer links, though they offer little control over selection, payment or forwarding. Pikvaa's Free plan has no monthly fee, but each event needs a storage pack, starting at ₹99 for 3 GB for 6 months before GST.

What do photographers use to share photos with clients?

Most use client gallery software, cloud storage or file transfer tools. Gallery software is built for the job, with proofing, selection, watermarks and download control. In India, Pikvaa combines a client gallery with family selection, part-payment tracking, crew and bookings in one studio app.

How do photographers save and store their photos?

Professionals follow a backup routine: keep memory cards until copies are verified, store originals on at least two separate drives, and keep one copy off-site or in the cloud. Client galleries such as Pikvaa handle delivery, but Pikvaa does not back up originals, so your own copies remain essential.

Where can photographers store thousands of photos?

Use external hard drives or a NAS for your archive, plus cloud storage for an off-site copy. For client delivery, per-event gallery storage works well: Pikvaa packs range from 3 GB to 120 GB per event, with optional Protected storage that keeps a verified second copy of final photos and clips.

What is photo proofing in photography?

Proofing is when clients review preview images, usually watermarked and lower resolution, and choose which ones to edit, print or put in an album. In Pikvaa, proofing happens in a PIN-protected gallery where the family tags photos, sets album priority and submits a locked selection by OTP.

What is a proof photo?

A proof photo is a preview version of an image, typically watermarked and lower resolution, shown to clients so they can choose favourites before final editing and delivery. Pikvaa galleries show web-quality proofs of about 0.4 MB, and full-quality finals are released only after payment.

What is photo selection in wedding photography?

Photo selection is when the couple and family choose which photos go into the edited album from the full set of shoot previews. A clear selection process avoids delays and disputes. Pikvaa supports joint family selection, album limits, priorities and an OTP-locked final list with version history.

How does a watermark work on photos?

A watermark is a logo or text placed over an image to show ownership and discourage unpaid use. Photographers add it to previews and remove it on final files. Pikvaa adds the watermark on its server to gallery previews by default, and the studio can turn it off per event.

How do I add a watermark to photos for free?

Many free editors and apps let you add a text or logo watermark manually. If you deliver through Pikvaa, you don't need to watermark each photo: gallery previews are watermarked automatically on the server by default, and final released files are clean.

How many photos should a wedding photographer deliver?

There's no fixed number; it depends on hours covered, number of events and the package. Agree the expected range in your quote. Delivering a well-chosen set and letting the family pick album photos online, as in Pikvaa, keeps quality high and selection quick.

What should a wedding photography package include?

A clear package lists events and hours covered, number of photographers and videographers, edited photo count or range, album size and pages, video deliverables, delivery timelines, advance and payment schedule, and cancellation policy. Pikvaa quotes let you build packages from templates and print the cancellation policy on every quote.

What should a wedding photography contract include?

Include the dates, venues and hours, deliverables and timelines, total price, advance and payment schedule, cancellation and date-change policy, usage rights and consent for portfolio use. Pikvaa quotes record the client's OTP acceptance with time and version, and accepted quotes are locked, giving you proof of what was agreed.

Do you pay a wedding photographer up front?

Usually an advance is paid up front to confirm the date, with the balance paid in parts before or after the wedding, often before final delivery. In Pikvaa, a booking stays Tentative until the studio marks the advance received, and final photos release only when the full balance is confirmed.

When should I book a wedding photographer?

Book as soon as your wedding date and venue are fixed, especially for peak wedding season, because popular photographers fill dates early. Confirm by paying the advance, and get a written quote with the cancellation policy. With Pikvaa, studios send quotes you accept by OTP.

How do I book a wedding photographer?

Shortlist photographers by portfolio and reviews, check date availability, compare package details, and ask for a written quote with payment and cancellation terms. Pay the advance to confirm. Studios using Pikvaa send a quote link you accept by OTP, and the booking is confirmed once the advance is recorded.

Can I cancel my wedding photographer and get a refund?

It depends on the cancellation policy in your quote or contract. Under Pikvaa's default policy, cancelling 60 or more days before refunds the advance minus 25%, 30 to 60 days refunds it minus 50%, and within 30 days there's no refund. Studios may set their own policy.

Build a portfolio, decide your niche, set clear packages and pricing, and register your business and GST when required. Create a simple website or landing page, get reviews, and set up a system for leads, quotes, payments and delivery. Pikvaa covers that system in one app with a Free plan.

How do I become a wedding photographer?

Learn your camera and lighting, assist or second-shoot for experienced studios, build a portfolio from real events, then offer clear packages. Organised delivery matters as much as shooting. Freelancers can join studios on Pikvaa for free and see only their assigned shoots and call sheets.

Photographers typically use editing software for raw processing and retouching, culling tools to pick the best frames, and client gallery or studio management software for delivery, bookings and payments. It depends on the number of events, guests and rituals happening at once. Larger weddings often use a team for candid, traditional and video coverage. What is candid photography?

Candid photography captures natural, unposed moments as they happen, rather than arranged poses. In Indian weddings, studios often pair a candid photographer with traditional coverage. Both sets can be uploaded to one Pikvaa gallery for the family to select from.

What is a pre-wedding photoshoot?

A pre-wedding shoot is a styled session of the couple before the wedding, often at an outdoor or themed location, used for invitations, save-the-dates and display at the reception. Pikvaa packs include up to 30 days of storage before the main shoot date, useful for sharing pre-wedding photos early.

Engagement photography covers the engagement ceremony or a couple session soon after it, capturing rituals, ring exchange, family and portraits. How long does it take to get wedding photos?

Delivery time depends on the studio's workload, edits and how quickly the family selects album photos. Agree timelines in the quote. Fast selection speeds things up: Pikvaa gives families a clear 60-day deadline with reminders, and the editor receives the list sorted by priority.

Why are wedding photographers so expensive?

Wedding coverage involves long hours across several events, multiple crew, expensive equipment, many hours of editing, album design and printing, and storage and delivery costs. Pricing also reflects experience and demand. Clear packages and transparent delivery help clients see the value.

How much does a wedding photographer cost in India?

Prices vary widely by city, experience, team size, events covered and deliverables like albums and video, so compare written quotes from a few studios. Ask what is included, how photos are delivered and selected, and the payment and cancellation terms before paying an advance.

What makes a good wedding photographer?

Look for a consistent portfolio, good reviews, clear packages, reliable communication and a professional delivery process. A portfolio is a curated collection of your best work that shows clients your style and quality. Use real client work with consent and update it regularly. Pikvaa's landing page lets studios show up to 20 photos on Free and 100 on Pro, only from client-consented galleries.

What equipment is needed for a photography studio?

A typical studio needs camera bodies, a range of lenses, lighting such as strobes or continuous lights, backdrops, memory cards, a computer for editing and reliable backup drives. Add a system for bookings and delivery so the business side runs smoothly.
