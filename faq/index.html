FAQ basics

What is Pikvaa?

Pikvaa is a studio app for Indian photographers that runs the whole job in one place: leads, quotes, bookings, crew, upload, client gallery and photo selection, payment records and final delivery. It is built for solo and small studios of two to five staff, works in Tamil and English, and is designed for low-end Android phones and slow networks.

Who is Pikvaa made for?

Pikvaa is made for small Indian photography studios, from solo photographers to teams of two to five staff, starting with Tamil Nadu. It suits wedding, engagement, pre-wedding, baby, birthday and event photographers who want client selection, payment tracking and delivery in one app instead of juggling Google Drive, WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

What can I do with Pikvaa as a photographer?

With Pikvaa you can capture leads, send quotes clients accept by OTP, confirm bookings, assign crew and freelancers, upload photos, share a PIN-protected gallery, collect the family's album selection, record client payments and release final photos only after payment. A Today screen shows only the tasks that need action, so nothing slips through.

Is Pikvaa a photo gallery app or a full studio management app?

Pikvaa is both. It covers the full studio workflow, from lead to quote, booking, shoot, upload, gallery, selection, payment, delivery and review. Studios that only need galleries can skip the CRM and use the direct path: create an event, buy a storage pack, share the gallery and deliver, with no quote or booking needed.
