What is the best studio management software for Indian wedding photographers?

Look for software that follows the Indian job: enquiries arriving on WhatsApp, quotes accepted quickly, advances that confirm a date, crews that include freelancers, part-payments by UPI and cash, and delivery tied to payment. Pikvaa is built around exactly that flow, with rupee pricing and Tamil support.

Leads & quotes

From enquiry to accepted quote in minutes.

Add a lead with quick add, by sharing a contact into Pikvaa, or from your studio landing page form. Move it through New and Talking, then send a quote as a PDF and link. The client accepts with an OTP, which gives you a clear record.

  • Quote validity with automatic expiry
  • Landing page templates for your studio
  • Duplicate leads flagged, never merged automatically

Bookings & crew

Dates and people, reserved together.

An accepted quote becomes a tentative booking with hold days. Mark the advance received and it confirms, reserving crew and shoot hours. Assign staff and invite freelancers per event.

  • Roles: Owner, Manager, Photographer, Editor or custom
  • Photographers don't see amounts or client phone numbers
  • Freelancers are free and never count as staff seats
