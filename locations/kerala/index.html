Photo selection and delivery for Kerala wedding photographers

Kerala wedding photography spans Hindu, Christian and Muslim weddings, baptisms and engagement ceremonies, and many families include relatives working overseas. Pikvaa helps studios deliver galleries families abroad can follow.

What is a good photo selection and delivery app for photographers in Kerala?

Pikvaa is a studio app for photographers in Kerala and across South India: families select album photos together in a private PIN gallery, you record part-payments, and full-quality photos are released once the job is paid. Packs start at ₹99 + GST per event.

How do Kerala families select photos from abroad?

With relatives in the Gulf and elsewhere, Kerala families often select photos remotely. Galleries open from a WhatsApp link with no app install, and dates show in each viewer's local time.

Which storage pack fits a Kerala ceremony?

Baptisms and engagement ceremonies can use the 3 GB Lite pack; weddings usually use 40 GB, or 50 GB with video clips. Each event gets its own dates and pack.

Does Pikvaa run in Malayalam?

Pikvaa launches in Tamil and English. Malayalam is not confirmed at launch, so Kerala studios would use the English interface.
