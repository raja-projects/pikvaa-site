What is a good photo selection and delivery app for photographers in Madurai?

Pikvaa is a studio app for photographers in Madurai and across Tamil Nadu: families select album photos together in a private PIN gallery, you record part-payments, and full-quality photos are released once the job is paid. Packs start at ₹99 + GST per event.

When elders choose the album

Selection often waits until the right relatives are together. With Pikvaa, the family opens one link with one Client PIN on their own phones and works on the same live selection - every removal shows who made it, with Undo, so no one's choice is lost.

Album priority for the designer

Clear deadlines

The 60-day selection deadline is visible to the family, and you can reopen for up to 14 days at a time if a key relative was travelling.

Yes. The main client can make up to 10 share galleries with view-only PINs.
