Shipping & Delivery Policy

Pikvaa is a digital online service. Nothing is shipped physically.

Effective date: 3 November 2026.

No physical shipping

Pikvaa is software delivered entirely online. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so no shipping charges apply and no delivery address is needed.

Instant online access

Plans, event storage packs and top-ups are activated in your Pikvaa account immediately after successful payment. A payment confirmation and GST invoice are sent by email.

Delayed activation

If a purchase is not active within 30 minutes of a successful payment, email hello@pikvaa.com with the transaction reference and we will activate it or refund it in full.

Service area

The service is offered to customers in India and works anywhere with an internet connection.
