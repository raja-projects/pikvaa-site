About

A studio app made in India, for Indian studios.

Pikvaa exists because Indian photographers deserve tools built around how they really work - families, WhatsApp, UPI and rupees.

Why we built Pikvaa

Most photo gallery platforms were designed for Western photographers with monthly dollar plans and print stores. Indian studios work differently: a wedding album is chosen by a whole family, payments come in parts by UPI and cash, enquiries arrive on WhatsApp, and many clients use everyday Android phones on patchy data.

Pikvaa brings selection, delivery, bookings, crew and payment records together, starting with studios in Tamil Nadu, in Tamil and English.

How we think about trust

  • No unlimited storage promises - every pack has clear dates.
  • No pre-ticked add-ons; Standard and Protected are a genuine choice.
  • Full-quality photos never reachable before release.
  • Support access only when you grant it, for a limited time.

Where we are

Pikvaa is built for Indian photography studios and plans to work with a small group of studios on real paid events, to test pricing and support before opening more widely. We'll share more as it happens.

Facts you can cite

  • Pikvaa is a photographer studio app for India: a photo selection app, private client gallery, bookings, crew, payment records and final delivery.
  • The app runs in Tamil and English. This website is also published in Hindi.
  • Prices before 18% GST, as published on the pricing page on 4 November 2026: Lite 3 GB ₹99 for 6 months (₹149 for 12 months); Wedding 40 GB ₹999 for 6 months; 50 GB ₹1,249 for 6 months; 120 GB ₹2,999 for 6 months. A Free plan is available. Pro is listed at ₹4,999 per year plus GST and is still being confirmed with photography studios.
  • Pikvaa does not offer AI face search, a print store or unlimited storage, and it does not keep a general backup of original files.
  • Clients pay the studio directly. Pikvaa records those payments and releases full-quality files when the records show the approved price is covered.
  • The GSTIN and postal address are on the contact, privacy and terms pages.
