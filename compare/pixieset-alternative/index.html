Is Pikvaa a good Pixieset alternative for Indian photographers?

Yes, if your priority is the Indian wedding workflow. Pikvaa prices per event in rupees (from ₹99 + GST), lets the whole family select album photos together in one live PIN gallery, and releases finals when payments are recorded. Pixieset is a strong choice if you want an integrated online store and photographer website and are comfortable with USD subscription plans.

Where Pikvaa differs

Rupee pricing, per event. Pixieset's plans are monthly subscriptions in US dollars. Pikvaa charges once per event in rupees, with GST shown, so a small birthday shoot and a three-day wedding are priced differently — and you never pay for an idle month.

Selection is a family decision. In Indian weddings the album list often involves parents and relatives. Pikvaa lets everyone with the Client PIN work on one live selection with album priorities, and shows who removed what.

Payment-linked delivery. Pikvaa does not process client payments. You record UPI or cash part-payments, and full-quality downloads unlock when the approved price is covered.

Where Pixieset may suit you better

If you sell prints and products online, want an integrated photographer website, or work mainly with international clients billed in dollars, Pixieset's store-centred approach is designed for that. Pikvaa has no print store.

Switching

You can start new events on Pikvaa while finishing existing galleries elsewhere. See pricing and the selection feature.

FAQs

How is Pikvaa different from Pixieset?

Pixieset is a polished all-in-one client gallery and online store platform built mainly for Western photographers, with monthly or yearly plans. Pikvaa is built for Indian studios: per-event packs in rupees from ₹99 before GST, Tamil and English, WhatsApp sharing, joint family selection with OTP submit, UPI payment records and payment-linked release.

How does Pikvaa compare with Pic-Time and ShootProof?

Pic-Time is a sales and print-focused gallery platform and ShootProof combines galleries with contracts and invoices; both are built for Western markets with monthly plans. Pikvaa is built for Indian studios, with per-event rupee packs, Tamil and English, WhatsApp sharing, family album selection and UPI payment records. Check their sites for current features.

Does Pikvaa have a free plan like Pixieset?

Pikvaa has a Free plan for creating events, recording payments and delivering, plus a 30-day Pro trial. Storage packs are always paid, from ₹99 before GST.
