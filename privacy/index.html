Privacy Policy

What information Pikvaa handles, why it is used and the choices available to users.

Effective date: 8 November 2026.

This policy explains how Tanishkha, the proprietor of Pikvaa ("we"), handles personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

Our role and the studio's role

For studio accounts, billing and our website, we decide how data is used. For client and event records, the studio decides what to upload and who gets access, and we process that data on the studio's instructions. Studios must give their clients any notice and obtain any consent their photography work requires.

Information we handle

  • Studio account details: name, business name, phone, email, GSTIN and staff or freelancer roles.
  • Client and guest details entered by a studio or client: names, phone numbers, emails, event details and guest PINs.
  • Photos, selections, favourites, comments and gallery activity.
  • Quotes, bookings, invoices and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are collected by Razorpay, not stored by Pikvaa.
  • Support messages, device and browser information, IP address, sign-in sessions and security logs.

How we use it

To provide accounts and role-based access, run event workflows, process and deliver photos, record selections, send OTPs and service messages, support bookings and payments, issue GST invoices, provide support, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and we do not use client photos for advertising or to train AI models.

Service providers

We share only what is needed with providers who help run Pikvaa: cloud hosting and storage, Razorpay for payments, Fast2SMS for SMS and OTPs, Resend for email, and monitoring and support tools. Some providers may store data outside India under contractual safeguards. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences and protect against misuse. If we add analytics or marketing cookies, we will ask first.

Retention and deletion

Event photos are kept until the deletion date shown on the event screen. Studio account data is deleted or anonymised within 90 days after an account is closed, after the 30-day download window. Invoices and payment records are kept for 8 years for tax law, and security logs for up to 1 year, unless a longer period is required for a dispute or by law.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, PIN-protected galleries and audit logs. No system is completely secure. Protect your credentials and gallery PINs, and keep original photographs separately. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to access a summary of your data, correct or update it, delete it, withdraw consent, or nominate a person to act for you. Write to hello@pikvaa.com. We may verify your identity and, for event photos, may pass the request to the studio that controls the event. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already done.

Children

Pikvaa accounts are for adults. Studios must obtain a parent's or guardian's consent before uploading or sharing photos of children, and we do not knowingly track or target children.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date and notified to studios before they take effect.
