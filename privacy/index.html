Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and your clients' photos.

This website and the Pikvaa app are operated by Tanishkha (Pikvaa is a product of Tanishkha), Ground Floor, 3-158, Middle Street, Swamythoppu, Kamarajapuram, Suchindrum, Kanniyakumari, Tamil Nadu - 629704, India. On this page, "Pikvaa", "we" and "us" mean that business.

What we collect

Studio account details (name, phone, email, GST number), event and client records you enter, photos you upload, payment records, and device and session data needed to keep the service secure.

How we use it

To provide photo selection, delivery and studio-management features, process payments, send service messages (including OTP and transactional SMS), provide support and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

How photos are handled

Previews are watermarked by default. Full-quality files are reachable only after the studio releases them, through a controlled download gateway.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI or bank credentials.

Retention

After a storage pack expires, files are archived for 30 days, then held in a 7-day recovery bin; live files are deleted by day 37 and removed from backups by day 67. Audit logs are kept for 1 year; payment and invoice records for 8 years, as required for tax purposes (these contain no photos).

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by emailing us. Clients of a studio can request deletion; verified requests are processed after review.

Support access

Our team can access a studio account only when the studio grants it, for 1 hour, 24 hours or 3 days.

Security and breaches

We use access controls and encryption in transit. If a breach occurs, we follow a written response plan and notify affected users and authorities as required by law.
